# Mobile-Sales-Dashboard
Mobile Sales Dashboard, an interactive and data-driven project designed to deliver meaningful business insights. This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of mobile sales performance across different brands, regions, and time periods.

An interactive **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to monitor and analyze mobile phone sales performance across brands, cities, payment methods, customer ratings, and time periods. This dashboard provides actionable insights that help businesses understand market trends, customer behavior, and brand performance.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The dashboard delivers a comprehensive view of mobile sales operations by tracking key performance indicators such as total sales, quantity sold, transactions, customer ratings, payment preferences, and brand-wise performance. Interactive filters enable users to explore data dynamically and make informed business decisions.

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 📍 Geographic Sales Analysis

* Interactive map displaying sales across major cities in India.
* Helps identify high-performing and low-performing markets.
* Enables region-wise sales comparison.

### 📅 Monthly Sales Analysis

* Tracks quantity sold month by month.
* Identifies seasonal demand patterns and sales fluctuations.
* Useful for forecasting and inventory planning.

### 📱 Brand Performance Analysis

* Compare sales across major smartphone brands:

  * Apple
  * Samsung
  * OnePlus
  * Vivo
  * Xiaomi
* Quickly identify market-leading brands.

### ⭐ Customer Rating Analysis

* Visual representation of customer satisfaction levels.
* Tracks distribution of ratings from 1 to 5 stars.
* Helps evaluate product and service quality.

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

* Sales breakdown by payment type:

  * UPI
  * Debit Card
  * Credit Card
  * Cash
* Provides insights into customer payment preferences.

### 📈 Sales by Day Analysis

* Tracks sales performance across days of the week.
* Identifies peak sales days for better promotional planning.

### 📋 Detailed Transaction Table

* Displays:

  * Brand Name
  * Mobile Model
  * Transactions
  * Total Sales
* Enables detailed product-level analysis.

### 🎛 Interactive Filters

* Brand-wise selection buttons.
* Month-wise navigation panel.
* Dynamic filtering for customized analysis.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

* Apple and Samsung contribute the highest share of total sales.
* UPI is one of the most preferred payment methods among customers.
* Customer ratings are predominantly positive, indicating strong customer satisfaction.
* Monthly sales show consistent demand throughout the year with peak periods in specific months.
* Major metropolitan cities contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Weekend sales tend to outperform weekday sales.
